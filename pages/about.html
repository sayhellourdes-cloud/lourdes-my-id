About Lourdes

More than flowers.

A personal space for flowers, stories, quiet moments, and beautiful details.

01

The Story

The beginning of Lourdes.

Some things begin with a plan. Others begin with a feeling.

Lourdes began with a quiet fascination for flowers and the emotions they carry. A flower can mark a beginning, accompany a celebration, or simply make an ordinary day feel a little more beautiful.

From that simple thought, Lourdes became a space for stories — a place where flowers, memories, ideas, and small details can have a place of their own.

Our Philosophy

Beauty in the little things.

We believe beauty does not always need to be grand.

Sometimes it is found in a single flower, the way sunlight falls across a room, a handwritten note, or a quiet moment shared with someone.

Lourdes is about noticing those moments — and giving them a place to stay.

02

The Future

Something is growing.

For now, Lourdes is a personal space.

A place to write, to collect, to create, and to appreciate beautiful things.

Perhaps one day, it will become something more — a flower studio, a creative space, or simply a place that continues to grow in its own way.

For now, we are happy to let it unfold naturally.
